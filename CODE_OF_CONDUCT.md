# Code of conduct

GGDS is built in the open by people from many institutions, suppliers and the public. Everyone taking part, in
issues, pull requests, reviews, research sessions and meetings, follows this code.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to GGDS, to GitHub, or to writing in English.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.
- Criticise ideas and work, not people. Explain why, and suggest what would be better.
- Make space for disabled people's experience: it is the evidence GGDS depends on.
- Respect people's privacy. Never share someone's personal details, or research participants' data, without their
  permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind, including by disability, ethnicity, region,
  religion, sex, gender, age, or political view.
- Sexual language or images.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a decision has been made and explained.
- Using GGDS spaces for political campaigning or advertising.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, or you are unsure, report it privately to the GGDS maintainers at NITA. Until NITA
publishes a contact address for GGDS, message an owner of the [ghana-gds](https://github.com/ghana-gds) GitHub
organisation privately. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainers may remove comments, lock discussions, or block people from the project. They will tell the person
reported what they did and why, unless that would put someone at risk.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
