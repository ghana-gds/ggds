Make a complaint about a service provider

Use this service if a company we regulate has not solved a problem you told it about.

You must complain to the company first. If it has not replied within 10 working days, or you are not happy with its reply, you can complain to us.

Before you start

You will need:

  • the name of the company
  • the date the problem started
  • the reference number the company gave you, if you have one

It takes about 10 minutes. You can do it on a phone.

Start now

What happens after you complain

We reply within 10 working days. We will tell you your reference number when you send your complaint.
