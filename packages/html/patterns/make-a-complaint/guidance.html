A short online complaint, built on the form pattern, that ends with a reference number and a clear promise of what happens next.

When to use

  • When people can complain to you about your own service, or about a company or provider you regulate.

When not to use

  • For general questions. Send people to your contact page.
  • For reporting fraud or a crime, when another body handles it. Say who does, and link to them.

How it works

It follows the ask for information pattern, with a page for each step.

  1. Start page. What people can complain about, who can complain, what they need before they start, how long it takes, and how long you take to reply. One "Start now" button.
  2. Question pages. What the complaint is about, what happened, what they would like you to do, and how to contact them.
  3. Check your answers in a summary list, each with a "Change" link, then the button that sends it.
  4. Confirmation. A confirmation panel with the reference number, what happens next and by when, and what people can do if they are not happy with your reply.

The start page

  • Say what you can and cannot deal with. If people must first complain to the provider, say so, and say how long to wait for its reply.
  • List what people need: a receipt, an account or reference number, the date it happened. Do not ask for a Ghana Card number unless you need it.
  • Say how long the form takes, such as "about 10 minutes", and that it works on a phone.
  • Give another way to complain, by phone or at an office, for people who cannot use the form.

Questions

  • Ask what happened in a text box, in the person's own words. Do not make them pick a category they may not understand first.
  • Ask what they would like you to do: a refund, a correction, an apology, or for the problem to stop.
  • Ask how they want you to contact them. Many people prefer a phone call or SMS to email.
  • Let people attach a photo or a receipt if it helps, but never require it: uploads are slow and costly on mobile data.
  • Do not ask people to repeat what they have already told you.

Confirmation and after

  • Show the reference number, such as EX-2026-00123, and send it by SMS or email.
  • Say what happens next, step by step, and how many working days each step takes.
  • Keep your promise. If a reply will be late, tell people before the date passes.
  • Say what people can do if they are not happy with your reply, such as asking for a review.

Accessibility

  • Follow the accessibility advice in the ask for information pattern: a unique page title for each step, the question as the heading, and a Back link.
  • Do not time people out while they write. Describing a problem can take a long time.
  • Do not limit the text box to a few characters. If you must set a limit, say what it is in the hint.
