Take a fee by mobile money or card: say what it is for, let people approve it on their phone, say clearly if it did not work, and give a receipt.

When to use

  • When people pay a fee, a fine, a licence or a renewal as part of a service.

When not to use

  • To ask people to pay into an account and upload proof. Take the payment in the service, so you know at once that it arrived.

How it works

  1. What you are paying: the amount, what it is for and who it is paid to, then how to pay: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money or a bank card.
  2. The number: the mobile money number, prefilled if you have it. Never the PIN.
  3. Approve on your phone: people approve the request on their phone. The page checks by itself, and says what to do if no request arrives.
  4. Paid: a confirmation panel with the receipt number, and the receipt as a summary list they can print or save. Send it by SMS too.
  5. Not paid: a page that says the payment did not go through, that no money was taken, and how to try again.

The amount

  • Write amounts as GH₵ 150.00: the symbol, a space and two decimal places. In sentences, "150 Ghana cedis" reads better for screen readers.
  • If people choose the amount (a donation, paying part of a fine), use a text input with the GH₵ prefix and inputmode="decimal".
  • Show any fee the payment provider charges before people pay, not after.

Mobile money

  • Never ask for a mobile money PIN on a website. People enter it on their phone, where the network asks for it.
  • Say which number the request went to, how long people have to approve it, and that the payment is to your institution by name: that is what the phone will show.
  • If no request arrives, offer to send it again, or to pay another way. Do not ask people to dial a code unless your provider requires it.
  • Do not take the money twice: give each payment a reference, and check it before you send a new request.

Error messages

  • "Your payment did not go through. No money was taken from your account." Then what to do.
  • Too little balance: "There was not enough money in your mobile money account. Top up and try again, or pay another way."
  • Timed out: "You did not approve the payment in time. Try again."

Accessibility

  • When the page checks for the payment by itself, tell screen readers when it changes, and give a button to check now as well.
  • The receipt is text, never only an image, so people can read it aloud, copy it and print it.
