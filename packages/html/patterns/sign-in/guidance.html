Sign people in with their phone number and a password, check it is them with a code by SMS, and keep their answers when the session ends.

When to use

  • When people need to come back to something that is theirs: an application in progress, a business account, their licences.

When not to use

  • To do a one-off task such as reporting a problem or paying a fee: let people do it without an account, and send a reference by SMS.
  • Only to collect contact details. An account is one more password to remember.

How it works

  1. Sign in: a phone number (or email address) and a password with a Show button, on one page.
  2. Check it is them: a 6-digit code by SMS, which their phone can fill in. Offer email as another way.
  3. While signed in: a timeout warning on every page, 2 minutes before the end of the session.
  4. Signed out: a page that says why, and that their answers are saved.

The sign-in page

  • Ask for what people already use with you: most people in Ghana have a phone number before an email address.
  • Use autocomplete="tel" (or email) and autocomplete="current-password", so password managers and phones can fill them in.
  • Link to "Forgotten your password?" and to "Create an account" under the button, never above it.
  • Never ask for a Ghana Card number and date of birth as a way to sign in: they are not secret.

Error messages

  • Empty: "Enter your phone number", "Enter your password".
  • Wrong: "The phone number or password is not right". Do not say which, or people can find out who has an account.
  • Too many tries: "You have tried too many times. Try again in 15 minutes, or reset your password". Say how long.
  • Code: "The code is not right" and "The code has expired. Send a new code".

Accessibility

  • Never block pasting into the password or the code, and never ask people to solve a puzzle to sign in (WCAG 3.3.8, accessible authentication).
  • Give people at least 20 minutes of inactivity before signing them out, warn them, and let them extend it (WCAG 2.2.1).
