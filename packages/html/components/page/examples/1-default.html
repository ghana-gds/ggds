An official website of the Government of Ghana How to tell

Check the address

The official address of this website is www.example.gov.gh. Most government websites end in .gov.gh, but some official websites do not. You can check it in the register of official government websites.

A padlock is not proof

A padlock or https:// before the address means your connection to the website is private. It does not show who runs the website.

We will never ask for your PIN

Government officials will never ask for your PIN, password or mobile money code, by phone, by SMS or on a website.
