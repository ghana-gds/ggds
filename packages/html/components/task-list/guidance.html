The sections of a long application with the status of each, so people can do them in any order and come back later.

When to use

  • For an application that takes more than one sitting, or has sections people can do in any order, such as a licence or a permit with several documents to upload.
  • When people need to see what is left before they can send it.

When not to use

  • For a short form that people finish in one go. Ask one question per page, with progress if it helps.
  • To show the stages of something your institution does after people apply. Use a timeline.

How to use

  • Put the summary first: "You have completed 2 of 5 sections." Then the list, then a button to check answers and send, once every section is complete.
  • Name each section by what people do or give: "Your details", "Upload your documents", "Pay the fee".
  • Give each section one status, as text: Completed, In progress, Not started or Cannot start yet. Use the tag colours as in the example. Only completed is green.
  • Link every section people can open. A section that cannot start yet (such as paying before the details are done) has no link, and its hint says why.
  • Give each link aria-describedby naming its status, so screen readers hear "Your details, Completed".
  • Save each section as people finish it, so they can leave and come back.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • The whole row is the target, at least 44px tall, while the link text stays the link's name.
  • Status is in words, never colour alone.

What you need to do

  • Link each section's status to its link with aria-describedby.
  • Keep the summary up to date: it is the first thing people read when they come back.
