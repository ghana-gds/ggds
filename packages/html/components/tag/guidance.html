A short label that shows a status, such as "Open" or "Closed".

When to use

  • To show the status of an item in a list or on a page: a licence, a consultation, an application.
  • When people need to scan many items and pick out the ones in a certain state.

When not to use

  • As a link, button or filter. Tags have no hover or focus style, so people will not know they can press them.
  • For a message people must read or act on. Use a notice.
  • For categories or keywords. Write them as plain text or links.

How to use

  • Use one to three words, such as "In force" or "Closing soon".
  • Choose the style by meaning: ggds-tag--success for a good or settled state ("Approved", "In force"), ggds-tag--info for something new or open ("New", "Open for comment"), ggds-tag--warning for something that needs attention soon ("Closing soon", "To be confirmed"), ggds-tag--danger for something stopped or refused ("Revoked", "Rejected"), and plain ggds-tag when the state is neither good nor bad ("Closed", "Draft").
  • Use the same word and style for the same status everywhere on your website.
  • Put the tag next to the thing it describes, such as after a title.
  • Use only one tag per item. If an item needs several, the status is not clear yet.

Do

  • Use words that make sense alone: "Closing soon".
  • Keep a short list of statuses and use it everywhere.

Don't

  • Rely on colour: a red tag saying "Status" tells people nothing.
  • Write a sentence in a tag.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • Every tag's text colour is checked against its background for at least 4.5:1 contrast by the GGDS tests (WCAG 1.4.3).
  • Tags are bold and have a border in the text colour, so they keep their shape even where background colours are not shown.

What you need to do

  • Let the words carry the meaning; the colour only supports them (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Check the tag reads well in order: a screen reader reads it as plain text with the words around it. If it is unclear, add visually hidden text such as <span class="ggds-visually-hidden">Status: </span>.
  • Do not make a tag a link or button.
