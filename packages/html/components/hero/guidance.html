The top of a homepage, in your institution's colour. It asks visitors what they need.

When to use

  • Once, on the homepage.

When not to use

  • On inner pages. They start with breadcrumbs and a heading.
  • For announcements. Use a notice.

How to use

  • Purpose line (gold): one sentence on what your institution does.
  • Heading: a question about the visitor's need.
  • Search: a normal search form. It works without JavaScript.
  • Common tasks: up to six links. On phones they form one row that scrolls sideways.
  • A ministry that searches all its agencies can use ggds-hero--hub, and tag each task with the agency that handles it.
  • Drawing (optional): add ggds-hero--art and put an SVG in ggds-hero__art with aria-hidden="true". Use the shared animated drawing (ggds-hero-art, below) or your institution's own. It shows beside the text on wide screens and is hidden on phones. It only moves for people who have not asked their device for less motion.

Do

  • Write tasks as things people do: "Make a complaint".

Don't

  • Use department names as tasks.
  • Add a photo slideshow.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • Every theme is checked so white text and the gold purpose line stay readable.
  • The search works without JavaScript, and its button keeps the word "Search" for screen readers when only the icon shows.
  • The drawing is hidden from screen readers, is left out on phones, and moves only for people who have not asked for less motion.

What you need to do

  • Write the heading as a real <h1>.
  • Give the search box a label (it can be visually hidden).
  • Add aria-hidden="true" to your own drawing.
