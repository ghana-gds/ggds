Who runs the website, how to contact them, the main sections and the links every government website must have.

When to use

  • On every page, at the bottom.

When not to use

  • As a place for news or promotions.

How to use

  • On the left: logo, name, what the institution does, address, email and phone.
  • On the right: each main section with up to five of its most-used pages.
  • Along the bottom: the copyright line, and at least Accessibility, Privacy and Disclaimer. NITA's standards ask every government website for these.

Accessibility

  • White text on every theme colour meets the strictest contrast level (7:1).
  • Every link is at least 44px tall.
  • Each section's links are a separate navigation area, named after the section.
