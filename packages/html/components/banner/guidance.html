Tells people the website belongs to the Government of Ghana, and how to check.

When to use

  • On every page of a government website, before the header.
  • On test, preview and copied versions of a site, use the "not official" version (ggds-banner--unofficial).

When not to use

  • On websites that are not run by a government institution.

How to use

  • Put your website's real address in "Check the address".
  • Link "register of official government websites" to the official list, when one is available.
  • The wording "An official website of the Government of Ghana" is still waiting for approval, including for state-owned companies.

Do

  • Name your website's real address.

Don't

  • Say that every government website ends in .gov.gh. Some official websites do not.

Accessibility

  • It opens and closes with the keyboard and works without JavaScript.
  • It scrolls away with the page, so it does not take space on small screens.
