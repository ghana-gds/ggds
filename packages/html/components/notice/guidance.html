A message box for information, success, a warning or danger, and an urgent notice for the whole site.

When to use

  • For something people must notice on this page.
  • The urgent notice: for something that affects everyone right now, such as a service outage.

When not to use

  • For form errors. Use the error summary.
  • For every paragraph. If everything stands out, nothing does.

How to use

  • Pick the type that fits: information, success, warning or danger.
  • Keep it short. Say what happened and what to do next.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • Each type has its own colour and a thick edge, and every type's text colour is measured against its background every time GGDS is built.

What you need to do

  • Start each notice with a word that says its type, such as "Warning:". It can be hidden on screen, but the meaning must never depend on colour alone.
  • Use role="alert" only for a notice that appears after the page has loaded.
