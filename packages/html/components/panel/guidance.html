The top of the page people see when they have finished a task: that it worked, and their reference number.

When to use

  • On the confirmation page at the end of a service: after people send an application, a complaint, a payment or a booking.

When not to use

  • For any other message: use a notice. A green panel means "you have finished", and it loses that meaning if it appears elsewhere.
  • For a task that did not work: show the problem and what to do, never a panel.

How to use

  • Make the panel's title the page's <h1>, and say what happened in a few words: "Complaint sent", "Payment received", "Application submitted".
  • Give the reference number in the panel, in ggds-panel__reference, if people may need it later. Keep references short, and avoid letters that look like numbers (O and 0, I and 1).
  • Under the panel, say what happens next and when: "We will reply by SMS within 10 working days". Then say how to get help, with the reference.
  • Say whether you sent a confirmation by SMS or email. Many people will not have a printer: tell them to write the reference down or take a screenshot.
  • Do not ask people to do anything else in the panel. Put any next step, such as paying a fee, below it with a button.
  • Do not use the browser's Back button to return to the form: the confirmation page is the end. Show the panel once, and send people who reload it to the same page, not to a second submission.

Do

  • Write "Your reference number" and the reference on its own line.
  • Say what happens next, under the panel.

Don't

  • Write "Success!" or "Thank you for your submission".
  • Use the panel for warnings or general information.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • White text on the success green has 6.6 to 1 contrast, checked by GGDS's tests (WCAG 1.4.3).
  • The reference breaks onto a new line rather than making a 320px screen scroll sideways (WCAG 1.4.10).
  • In Windows contrast themes the panel keeps a border, and when printed it prints as black text in a box.

What you need to do

  • Make the panel's title the page's only <h1>, and start the page <title> with it too, so screen reader users hear straight away that it worked.
  • Do not rely on the green colour: the words must say it worked (WCAG 1.4.1).
