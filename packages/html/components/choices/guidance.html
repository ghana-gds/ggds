Let people choose one option (radios) or several (checkboxes) from a short list.

When to use

  • For up to about eight options.

When not to use

  • For long lists. Use a select field.
  • For a single yes/no agreement that could be a button.

How to use

  • Group the options in a <fieldset> with the question in its <legend>.
  • On error, put the message after the question and connect it with aria-describedby.
  • In the error summary, link to the first option.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • The radios and checkboxes are enlarged, and the whole row can be tapped.
  • They are the browser's own inputs, so they work with every assistive technology.

What you need to do

  • Group the options in a <fieldset> with the question in its <legend>.
  • On error, put the message after the question, connect it with aria-describedby, and link the error summary to the first option.
