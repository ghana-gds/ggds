Links to move between pages of a long list.

When to use

  • For search results, news and other long lists.

When not to use

  • To move between steps of a form. Use a "Continue" button.

How to use

  • Show previous and next links, and numbered pages in between.
  • Mark the current page with aria-current="page".

Accessibility

  • Name the navigation by what is being paged, such as "Pages of results".
