Your institution's logo and name, the main sections and search, at the top of every page.

When to use

  • On every page, straight after the banner.

When not to use

  • For links within one section. Use tab links or a list on the page instead.

How to use

  • Use your approved logo. Until you have one, use your initials in the round badge (ggds-header__monogram).
  • If your name is longer than 45 characters, add ggds-header__name--long. It sets the name smaller on phones.
  • Mark the section the visitor is in with aria-current="page".
  • Point Search at your website's search page.
  • Below 1280px wide, the sections move behind a menu button. The menu pushes the page down instead of covering it. Without JavaScript the sections show as a list under the name.

Do

  • Keep to six sections or fewer, with short names.
  • Name sections after what people do: "Services", "Complaints".

Don't

  • Name sections after departments people do not know.
  • Add a second row of links to the header.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • The current section is shown by more than colour: a line beside it on phones and an underline on wide screens.
  • The menu pushes the page down instead of covering it, so keyboard users never land on content they cannot see. Escape closes it and returns focus to the menu button.
  • The page leaves room under the sticky header, so focused links are never hidden behind it.
  • Without JavaScript the sections show as a list, so nothing depends on the script.

What you need to do

  • Mark the current section with aria-current="page".
  • Give a logo image alt text naming your institution, or use the initials, which are hidden from screen readers because the name follows.
  • Keep section names short so the menu stays easy to scan.
