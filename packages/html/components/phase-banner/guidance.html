Tells people a service is new and still being improved, and asks for their feedback.

When to use

  • On every page of a new online service while it is being tested with the public (alpha) or is open to everyone but still changing (beta).

When not to use

  • On a finished service, or on information pages that are not part of a new service.
  • To give news or warnings: use a notice.

How to use

  • Put it directly under the header.
  • Link "feedback" to a short form that asks what people were trying to do and what went wrong.
  • Remove it when the service is finished.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • The tag says "Alpha" or "Beta" in words, with white text on the government blue at more than 4.5 to 1.

What you need to do

  • Make sure the feedback form itself meets GGDS's accessibility rules, so everyone can tell you what went wrong.
