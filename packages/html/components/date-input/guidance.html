Three boxes for day, month and year, for dates people already know, such as their date of birth.

When to use

  • For dates people know or can find on a document: their date of birth, the date on a receipt, when something happened.

When not to use

  • For booking a date in the next few weeks: show the dates people can choose as radios, such as available appointment days.
  • When you only need a month and year, or a year: leave out the boxes you do not need.
  • With a calendar pop-up or the browser's date picker (type="date") for dates of birth: they are slow to use for a date 30 years ago, look different in every browser, and are hard to use with some screen readers.

How to use

  • Ask the question in the <legend>, and give an example in the hint, written the way people type it: "For example, 27 3 1990".
  • Label each box: Day, Month, Year. Use inputmode="numeric" so phones show the number keypad, and not type="number", which drops leading zeros and changes the value when people scroll.
  • For a date of birth, add autocomplete="bday-day", "bday-month" and "bday-year", so browsers can fill it in.
  • Accept what people type: "3" or "03" for March, and month names such as "March" or "Mar". Remove spaces before checking.
  • Give the year box ggds-date-input__input--year, so it is wider. The width shows people the format.
  • Show the date back in full, on a check page: "27 March 1990".

Error messages

Say what is wrong and how to fix it. Highlight only the boxes with the problem (aria-invalid="true"), and link the error summary to the first of them.

  • Nothing entered: "Enter your date of birth".
  • A part missing: "Date of birth must include a year".
  • Not a real date: "Date of birth must be a real date".
  • In the future: "Date of birth must be in the past".
  • A year with two digits: "Year must include 4 numbers".

Do

  • Accept "3", "03" and "March" for the month.
  • Keep what people typed when you show an error.

Don't

  • Use one box with a format such as DD/MM/YYYY: people type dates in many ways.
  • Move the cursor to the next box automatically: it surprises people and gets in the way of corrections.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • Each box is at least 44px tall, with 16px text, so phones do not zoom in.
  • The boxes wrap onto a new line on very narrow screens.

What you need to do

  • Put the boxes in a <fieldset> with a <legend>, and point the fieldset's aria-describedby at the hint and any error, so the question is read with every box (WCAG 1.3.1, 3.3.2).
  • Give each box its own visible label.
