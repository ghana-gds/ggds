The pages of one section of a website, beside its content, so people can move between them.

When to use

  • For a section with several pages that people read in any order, such as the pages about fees or about the institution.

When not to use

  • For the whole website: that is the header's job.
  • For the parts of one page: use a contents list.
  • For the steps of a task in order: people should follow the task one page at a time.

How to use

  • Put it in the side column of ggds-with-aside. On phones it comes after the content; on desktops it sits beside it.
  • Mark the current page with aria-current="page".
  • Show the pages under the current one in an indented list, one level deep at most.
  • Use the pages' own titles as link text.

Accessibility

What GGDS does

  • The current page is shown by a line, a tint and bold text, and is announced as the current page, so it never depends on colour alone.
  • Every link is at least 44px tall.

What you need to do

  • Give the navigation a label that says what it lists, if "In this section" is not clear.
  • Keep it after the main content in the HTML, so people using a screen reader reach the page's content first.
